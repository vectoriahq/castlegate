# Reporting a security problem

Report a security problem in Castlegate privately, through this repository's **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**. Please do not open a public issue for one.

Say what you sent, what came back and what you expected, and which QGIS Server version you ran it on. A check in `tests/server/run.mjs` or a unit test that shows it is the quickest way for us to reproduce it.

What Castlegate must hold to, and where to read closest, are in [`docs/security-review.md`](docs/security-review.md). What it is known not to protect is in [`docs/limits.md`](docs/limits.md).
